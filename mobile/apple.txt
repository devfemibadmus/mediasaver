Hello App Review,

We updated the app so it no longer supports arbitrary third-party media sources.

The app now only allows downloading media from official NASA sources that state the content is public domain or otherwise free to download, use, and redistribute unless otherwise noted.

Approved source:
- https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/

Supporting policy pages:
- NASA Scientific Visualization Studio FAQ: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/
  This page states that NASA SVS content is public domain unless otherwise noted and is free to download, use, and redistribute.
- NASA JPL Image Use Policy: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/
  This page states that images and video on JPL public websites may be used without prior permission unless otherwise noted.

The app does not support unauthorized third-party platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, SoundCloud, Vimeo, or similar services.

Please review the updated build.
